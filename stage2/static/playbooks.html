Playbooks

The four enforcement tiers keep running automatically on every window exactly as they do without any playbook configured. A playbook is a separate layer on top that starts when its trigger condition fires and runs a linear sequence of stages over time (escalate, notify, report); it never replaces or gates the tiers.
