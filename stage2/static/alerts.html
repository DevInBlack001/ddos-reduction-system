Alerting

Alerts fire on a victim's classification changing to/from DDoS, on every new hard block (Tier 1/2), and on the Tier 4 aggregate rate-limit fallback. Soft rate-limits (Tier 3) don't alert on their own, and a source already alerted stays suppressed for the configured block duration so a sustained attack doesn't spam either channel.
