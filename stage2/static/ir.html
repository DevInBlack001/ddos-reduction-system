Incident Response & Reporting

Export Raw Logs (CSV)
Downloads the entire SQLite audit ledger in comma-separated values format. Suitable for ingestion into corporate SIEM platforms (Splunk, ELK) or spreadsheet analysis.
Export Incident Report (PDF)
Generates a report covering event volume, packet rate and entropy against the current baseline, a plain-language breakdown of what happened during the window, and the active blocklist, built entirely from server-side state.
